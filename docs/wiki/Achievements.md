# Achievements

Long-horizon goals that track across a campaign.

## Design notes

- Definitions live in `src/data/achievements/*.json`.
- Each achievement has a predicate over game state and a one-shot unlock flag.
- Achievements are not resources; they do not feed back into simulation.
