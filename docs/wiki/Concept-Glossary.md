# Concept Glossary

Terms used in-game and in the docs.

- **Political Capital (PC)** — the primary spendable currency of
  influence. Generated by actions, events, and office; spent on whips,
  cards, and negotiations.
- **Radicalism** — a per-cohort propensity to leave normal politics.
  High radicalism gates extremist [[Events]] and alters [[Population]] mood.
- **Cohort** — an intersectional population slice (region, class, age,
  education, ideology). The smallest unit the simulation tracks.
- **Bill** — a draft law moving through the [[Legislation]] pipeline.
- **Committee** — a subset of [[Congress]] that reviews a bill before
  the floor.
- **Whip** — a Political Capital expenditure that shifts a specific
  member's vote probability on a specific bill.
- **Faction** — a sub-party grouping inside [[Congress]] with shared
  ideology or interest.
- **Scenario** — a starting configuration (see [[Scenarios]]).
- **Action** — a discrete player move that ticks the clock and spends
  resources.
- **Card** — a playable action drawn from a deck (see [[Cards]]).
- **Rarity** — every card carries one of six tiers (common, uncommon,
  rare, epic, legendary, prismatic) driving pack drop weights and
  visual treatment. See [[Cards]].
- **Card pack** — a sealed bundle of five cards purchasable in the
  Collection screen. Pack contents are deterministic per seed.
- **Action Points (AP)** — a weekly budget of discrete moves. Refills
  every Monday tick; some cards spend AP in addition to PC.
- **Extended Tooltip** — the Paradox-style hover panel that surfaces
  full term definitions, cross-links, and "see also" relations. Hold
  `Shift` while hovering to pin.
