## Scenario Brief

**Era:** United States, 1947–1991  
**Setting:** A legislature operating under permanent low-grade emergency — the spectre of communist expansion, domestic loyalty investigations, and an arms race that never formally starts and never formally ends.  
**Stakes:** Build a bipartisan consensus on containment policy, survive the Red Scare, and keep the government functioning through a half-century of proxy wars and domestic social upheaval.

**Opening situation:** You enter the chamber in a year the world is newly hostile and your own party is newly fractious. The ideological lines that held during the war no longer hold. Old alliances are liabilities.

**Central tensions:**
- Bipartisan coalition is both a necessity and a political risk
- Domestic security politics creates a permanent leverage threat from ideological enforcers
- The Whip structure is weaker; floor management is personal

**Named NPC archetypes planned:** Cold War Machine Boss, Anticommunist Enforcer, Bipartisan Pragmatist, Isolationist Old Guard  
**Hooked systems:** Faction system, Dialogue (M2), all legislation systems, Espionage-event layer (post-1.0 planned)

**Victory framing (draft):**  
> *"The agreement holds. For now. The world is still here."*

**Loss framing (draft):**  
> *"It collapsed the way most things do — incrementally, then all at once."*
