## Studies

- **Victoria III** — population modelling, lobby/interest-group design,
  data-driven events. See `docs/research/victoria-3.md`.
- **Crusader Kings III** — character-first storytelling, schemes,
  trait interactions. See `docs/research/crusader-kings-3.md`.
- **Anno 1800** — information hierarchy, KPI dashboards, onboarding
  complex systems. See `docs/research/anno-1800.md`.
- **Political simulation fidelity** — what real politics models get
  right and wrong for games. See `docs/research/political-simulation-fidelity.md`.
- **UI/UX patterns for grand strategy** — see `docs/research/ui-ux-patterns.md`.
- **Comparative systems matrix** — what each title teaches us for each
  of our systems. See `docs/research/comparative-systems.md`.

## How we use research

Each PR that introduces a system cites at least one research note when
a design decision is borrowed or explicitly rejected.
