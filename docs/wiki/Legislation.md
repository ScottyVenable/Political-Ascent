# Legislation

Bills are the primary lever the player uses to change the world.

## Lifecycle

1. **Draft** — choose a template or build from provisions.
2. **Committee** — a subset of the chamber reviews, amends, reports out or kills.
3. **Floor** — the full chamber debates and votes.
4. **Other chamber** — repeat committee and floor.
5. **Executive** — sign, veto, or pocket-veto.

## Design notes

- Provisions are data. Each has costs, effects on [[Population]] cohorts,
  and modifiers on [[Economy]] indicators.
- Whip actions ([[Congress]]) shift member vote probabilities; they do
  not force outcomes.
