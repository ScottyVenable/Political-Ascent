# Political Ascent

> **GDD reference:** §1, §3
> **Implementation status:** Implemented
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-02 (Vex, GDD v0.4-DRAFT)

You are a senator. You have a signature bill, a divided chamber, and one term to prove it matters.

*Political Ascent* is a hybrid RPG and political simulation. It puts you inside the machinery of American democracy — not as a passive observer, but as an agent making real choices with real consequences. Speeches actually move populations. Alliances actually determine survival. Compromises actually reshape who your character becomes.

There are no correct political outcomes. There are choices, consequences, and legacies.
