## Scenario Brief

**Era:** United States, 1861–1877  
**Setting:** The most consequential legislative session in American history. The Union is fractured or being rebuilt depending on which act you enter at. Constitutional amendments are live legislation. The rules of the chamber are not stable.  
**Stakes:** Pass foundational legislation that determines whether the war's end means anything. The failure conditions here are not political embarrassments — they are historical catastrophes.

**Opening situation:** You have entered a Congress that is simultaneously prosecuting a war and trying to govern a country that contains its own enemy. Half the chamber's normal seats are vacant or contested. Every procedural norm is contested alongside them.

**Central tensions:**
- The Radical Republican / moderate split mirrors the Modern America factional map but with existential stakes
- Military governors as extralegal actors with no representation
- Constitutional novelty: the 13th, 14th, and 15th Amendments are possible in-run legislative goals
- Reconstruction collapse is a failure path with a long fuse

**Named NPC archetypes planned:** Radical Reconstructionist, Pragmatic Unionist, Southern Redeemer, Freedmen's Bureau Advocate  
**Hooked systems:** Legislation (all amendments as bills), Congress procedural generation (contested seats), Factions, Events (assassination risk layer, post-1.0)

**Era register note:** portentous, formal, weight-of-history — no casual contractions in floor speeches.

**Victory framing (draft):**  
> *"The text is ratified. What it means will be argued for a century. You wrote the words."*

**Loss framing (draft):**  
> *"Reconstruction falls. The reasons will be studied. The consequences will not require study."*
