# Economy

A lightweight macro economy: indicators, sectors, and exogenous shocks.
It is deliberately not a full macro simulator — it is a feedback source
for [[Population]] mood and [[Events]].

## Indicators

- GDP growth
- Unemployment
- Inflation
- Inequality index
- Budget balance

## Sectors

- Agriculture, Manufacturing, Services, Public.

## Shocks

- Exogenous events (recession, energy spike) are injected through the
  [[Events]] system.
