> **GDD reference:** §12.7.1, §3.2, §3.3
> **Implementation status:** Implemented (shipped in current build)
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-02 (Vex, GDD v0.4-DRAFT)

## Overview

A first-term United States Senator. A 50-50 chamber. One signature bill. Six years to matter.

Modern America 2024 is the launch scenario — the context the game was designed around. Every system has been calibrated against it. When something works, this is where you will feel it first.

## Setting and Stakes (GDD §12.7.1)

**Era:** Contemporary United States, 2025–2031  
**Chamber:** United States Senate, 50-seat tiebreak  
**Opening situation:** You have been seated for three weeks. Your signature bill is drafted. Your party has the majority on paper. You are about to learn what a majority on paper actually means.

The chamber is politically exhausted and operationally fractious. Procedural obstruction is the default instrument of governance. Your bill will spend 42 days minimum in transit through committee, floor debate, and vote. During those 42 days, the world generates crises, your colleagues generate demands, and your opponents generate leverage. You have the same tools they do.

## Victory Conditions

| Condition | Weight |
|---|---|
| Signature bill passed and signed | **Required** |
| Re-elected with +5% margin | Optional |
| ≥60% national approval at re-election | Optional |
| Reach majority leader or higher by end of term | Optional |

A run ends in victory when the required condition is met. Optional conditions unlock additional victory text and achievement credit. A Pyrrhic victory is still a victory.

## Failure Conditions

| Condition |
|---|
| Lost re-election |
| Resigned for scandal |
| Party expulsion |
| Censured with all committee seats stripped |

Any one of these ends the run immediately.

## Central Tensions

- **Margin:** your majority is one seat. One defection on any bill is a loss.
- **Factions vs. coalition:** the faction system (M2) will make clear that your party is several parties under one tent.
- **Time vs. capital:** the 42-day bill clock creates genuine urgency; Political Capital is an accelerant but not a substitute for time.
- **Ambition vs. integrity:** the scandal flag is a game mechanic, not a metaphor. Integrity is tracked.

## How to Play It

**What to expect:** the first run will end before the vote. That is correct. Read what failed and why. The second run will fail differently. The third run you will understand what the game is asking.

**Where to start:** draft the simplest bill you can justify. Get it into committee. Read the committee member roster. Start one relationship before the bill needs it.

**Survival rule:** never assume a vote is safe. The Chamber rolls every legislator's ideology, personality, and relationship delta every turn. Votes that looked certain collapse. That is the game.
