## Scenario Brief

**Era:** United States, 1877–1901  
**Setting:** A Senate that is openly for sale. Machine politics at full operational capacity. The public does not yet know what it is allowed to want.  
**Stakes:** Pass meaningful reform against a chamber whose senior members have written the rules of the chamber to protect themselves. The economy system runs hot — corporate interests are a faction in every vote.

**Opening situation:** You are a new Senator in a chamber that runs on relationships measured in railroad contracts and tariff carve-outs. Your reform agenda is technically legal and practically unworkable. The task is to make it workable.

**Central tensions:**
- Machine boss networks are the dominant archetype; independent action is expensive
- Corporate interests as a non-voting faction with continuous leverage
- Populist movements building outside the chamber; can be used or destabilised
- Tariff legislation is the central fiscal mechanism; every vote traces back to it

**Named NPC archetypes planned:** Gilded Age Machine Boss, Corporate Caucus Whip, Agrarian Populist Outsider, Reform Muckraker Ally  
**Hooked systems:** Economy (corporate influence model), Legislation, Factions, Cards (Gilded Age-specific influence operations)

**Era register note:** florid public rhetoric masking private cynicism; no post-1960s idiom anywhere.

**Victory framing (draft):**  
> *"The act passes. The trust reads the headline and begins drafting the workaround."*

**Loss framing (draft):**  
> *"The machine ran exactly as designed. You were not the engineer."*
