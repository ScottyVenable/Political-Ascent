# Research: Anno 1800

**Reference:** Anno 1800 — Ubisoft Mainz, 2019
**Focus for us:** Population tiers with layered needs, satisfaction loops, dense UI that stays readable, diegetic news/notification feeds.

---

## 1. Summary

Anno 1800 is a city-builder, but its design lessons apply anywhere a game tracks **population happiness over many inputs**. Its population is stratified into **tiers** (Farmers → Workers → Artisans → Engineers → Investors). Each tier has **Basic Needs** and **Luxury Needs**. Meeting needs grows the tier; unmet needs shrink it or trigger unrest. Higher tiers unlock new buildings, goods, and political problems.

Anno's UX is the other reason it is worth studying: it is information-dense without being cluttered. Every tile, every person, every good has a tooltip; every problem has a one-click path to the place in the UI that fixes it.

---

## 2. Relevant mechanics

### 2.1 Population tiers and needs
- Each tier has a visible needs panel with two columns: **Basic** (growth-gating) and **Luxury** (happiness-boosting).
- Each need is one line: icon, name, "supplied/required", bar fill.
- Meeting all Basic needs allows growth; meeting Luxury increases happiness and tax revenue.
- Happiness is tier-specific, visualised per-tier with a clear face icon (content → angry).

### 2.2 Consumption loop
- Every tick, each population tier consumes goods proportional to its size.
- Under-supply is not a binary fail; happiness drops gradually; eventually population shrinks.
- Over-supply is invisible waste — no reward for excess.

### 2.3 City attractiveness
- A second stat separate from needs: **Attractiveness**. Driven by decorations, parks, monuments.
- High attractiveness attracts tourists and immigration.
- Conceptually: *functional needs* vs *aesthetic / aspirational needs*.

### 2.4 UI affordances worth copying
- **One-click navigation to problems.** When a building is unstaffed, the notification jumps the camera to it.
- **Statistics menu.** A dedicated "statistics" screen with tabs for Population, Production, Finance, Storage. Always accessible.
- **Newspaper.** Periodic in-world newspaper summarises major events, with flavour prose. Diegetic, not a popup list.
- **Diagnostic view modes.** Overlays that recolor the map by one metric at a time (coverage, income, etc.).

## 3. What translates to Political Ascent

| Anno 1800 concept | Our equivalent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Population tiers | `PopulationGroup[]` | Our groups are horizontal (labor, business, retirees, etc.) rather than vertical tiers, but the **needs model** is identical. |
| Basic vs Luxury needs | Group-specific metrics | Each group can have *Core Concerns* (unemployment, healthcare) and *Aspirational Concerns* (cultural recognition, international standing). |
| Happiness face icons | Group happiness readout | Use clear 5-step iconography, not just a number. |
| Attractiveness | Approval rating + National Mood | The macro mood is our Attractiveness — it is influenced by decorative / symbolic actions (speeches, monuments, symbolic laws). |
| One-click to problem | Alert-to-panel deep links | Every alert in our Dashboard must deep-link to the panel that can act on it. Non-negotiable UX rule. |
| Statistics menu | Economy Dashboard + Population panel | Combine into a single "Nation" overview with tabs, always one keystroke away. |
| Newspaper | News ticker + weekly briefing | Elevate the briefing modal to a stylised *broadsheet* layout with headlines and prose. |
| Diagnostic view modes | Congress chamber view toggles | We already plan this for the 535-seat grid (party / ideology / happiness / influence / vote prediction). Confirm: ship all five in v0.1. |

## 4. What does not translate

- **Tile-based construction.** We do not build buildings.
- **Trade routes / supply chains.** Our economy is macro, not per-good.
- **Multiplayer.** Out of scope.

---

## 5. Suggested hooks in our systems

- **PopulationGroup schema:** Add `coreConcerns: ConcernId[]` and `aspirationalConcerns: ConcernId[]`. Concerns are world metrics the group weighs heavily. A `Concern` is a tuple of (metric, threshold, weight). Happiness = weighted fulfillment of concerns. This is a direct Anno port and makes group reactions legible ("Retirees are unhappy because Healthcare Access is 54% and they require 70%").
- **Dashboard alerts:** Every alert must carry `{ panelId, selectionHint }` so clicking routes to the exact panel and pre-selects the relevant entity (bill, group, legislator).
- **News / briefing:** Convert weekly briefing into a stylised newspaper view: headline + subhead + pull-quote, with 3–5 stories. Pull quotes can be procedural ("Senator Ramos called the bill 'a betrayal of working families.'").
- **View-mode toggles:** Apply the Congress chamber pattern to the Population panel (recolour by happiness / radicalism / party lean / wealth) and Economy dashboard (recolour metrics by trend direction).

---

## 6. Open questions

- Should happiness be a **single group scalar** (simple, current design) or a **vector of concern fulfillment** summed into a scalar (Anno-style, richer tooltips)? Recommendation: vector internally, scalar externally. Low implementation cost, huge readability gain.
- Do we keep the weekly briefing as a **modal** (interrupts play) or a **drawer** (non-blocking)? Anno uses non-blocking for most updates. Recommendation: drawer for weekly, modal only for crises.
