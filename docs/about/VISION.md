# Vision — Political Ascent

Political Ascent puts the player inside the machinery of politics — not as a puppet of pre-authored outcomes, but as an agent inside a living, reactive simulation. Your speeches actually move populations. Your alliances actually determine your survival. Your compromises actually reshape who you become.

We aim for **authenticity over editorializing**. There is no correct political outcome; there are choices, consequences, and legacies.

## Pillars

1. **Political authenticity** — systems grounded in how legislation and governance actually function.
2. **Meaningful choice** — no filler decisions; ripple effects are visible.
3. **Living world** — the simulation runs whether you act or not.
4. **Personal narrative** — background, traits, relationships shape your story.
5. **Accessible depth** — layered onboarding; experts find depth, new players find footing.

## Non-goals (for MVP)

- Multiplayer.
- Full diplomacy or military model.
- Photo-realistic graphics.
- Partisan messaging.
